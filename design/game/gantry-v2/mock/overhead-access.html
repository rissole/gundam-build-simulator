Overhead rope access Gantry v2 mock. Two walkways at 20.6 m, each with its own lift. The workshop-side walkway is two hinged leaves on the same posts as the 8.4 m wings, split at the centreline, so they swing open for the crane like the wings below. The exit-side walkway is fixed. You walk out, swing a davit over the robot, clip in and lower yourself to the connection. Coloured blocks only; sizes are coarse.

Reach is measured from the worker's shoulders: up to 0.85 m is fine, up to 1.6 m works with a pull-in lanyard clipped to the robot, beyond that it's out of reach. The rope must stay clear of the robot blocks and at least 0.6 m from the crane's cables.
